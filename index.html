Yizhen Li (李奕臻)

Hi! I am a 2nd year PhD student at University of Virginia, advised by Leonid Petrov. Broadly speaking, I am interested in probability theory and its connections with mathematical physics and algebraic combinatorics. To be more specific, I am interested in universality classes, such as KPZ, in statistical mechanics, both the algebraic and analytic perspectives. Currently, I am studying integrable probability and exactly solvable models, e.g., six-vertex, random growth, and random tilings. Berfore UVa, I did my undergraduate studies at S.S.Chern class, Nankai University.
